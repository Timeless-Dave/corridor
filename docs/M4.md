# M4: company context, precedents, and routing

M4 verifies the company behavior implemented by the fixed M2 queries and M3 loop.
The read-only checker validates both seeded corridors, all six policy/document links,
and all current precedents. It needs FalkorDB credentials but no model key:

```bash
cd api
../.venv/bin/python -m data.check_company
CORRIDOR_LIVE_TESTS=1 ../.venv/bin/python -m unittest tests.test_company -v
```

The checker expects the original synthetic company seed. Deliberately changing that
seed, including adding newer decisions, requires updating its expected fixtures.
It does not reset the graph or create cases.

| Corridor | Owner | Assignee | Policies |
|---|---|---|---|
| US-NG | team_0 | emp_0 | POL-AML-1 through POL-AML-4 |
| NG-US | team_1 | emp_1 | POL-AML-5 and POL-AML-6 |

`corridor_context` retrieves the corridor's owning team, employee lead, policies,
and defining source documents. `precedents` follows BASED_ON and excludes every
decision with an incoming SUPERSEDES edge. The direction is new → old:
DEC-6 → DEC-0, so DEC-0 cannot be selected as a current precedent.
POL-AML-2 has two current precedents (DEC-7 and DEC-1); newer dates alone do not
supersede a decision without an explicit graph relationship.

`who_knows` ranks authors of decisions referenced by historical cases. It identifies
emp_6 for the seeded fan-in case. This is historical expertise, not assignment:
the lead of the selected corridor receives the new case.

The loop forces company context and precedent retrieval before its final decision.
The chosen policy must belong to that corridor, the decision must be among that
policy's current precedents, and the assignee must be its lead. Invalid model
references close an unrouted MONITOR for review. The write query independently
rechecks policy/decision relationships, lead ownership, and supersession immediately
before closure. If a decision becomes superseded after retrieval, closure using the
stale reference is rejected without partially closing or routing the case.
